Piano audio attribution

This app uses a compact subset of Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm.
The recordings are of a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

For this app, selected MP3 anchor samples are downloaded during the GitHub Actions
build from the @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity4 package and bundled inside the IPA.
Playback rate is changed by a small amount for notes between the recorded anchors.
